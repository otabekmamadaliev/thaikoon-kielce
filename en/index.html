555 reviews, a 4.6 rating — and not one website of its own

Thaikoon is at Paderewskiego 42 in Kielce. 555 reviews at 4.6 puts it among the stronger restaurants in the city.

Thaikoon’s menu is currently only available through Pyszne.pl and Facebook. Every order placed through an aggregator costs the restaurant commission.

A site of its own with the menu and a phone number will not replace the aggregator, but it gives a guest a second route: ring directly. That route costs no commission.
